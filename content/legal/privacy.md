# Privacy Policy — RennovAIte

> **DRAFT FOR LEGAL REVIEW — 29 Sep 2026.** Prepared as a working draft aligned to UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL) and common practice among SaaS/construction-tech platforms. **Not legal advice; must be reviewed and finalised by qualified UAE counsel before publication.** Bracketed items need your input.

*Last updated: [date]*

**Who we are.** RennovAIte ("RennovAIte", "we", "us") is a renovation planning platform operated by **[legal entity name, licence no., registered address, Dubai, UAE]**, available at rennovaite.fit and associated applications (the "Platform"). This policy explains what data we collect, why, and your rights. Contact: **[privacy@rennovaite.fit]**.

## 1. Data we collect

**Account data.** Email address and sign-in records (we use passwordless email links; we do not store passwords).

**Project data.** Content you upload or create to use the Platform: floor plans and drawings, site photographs, measurements, room and zone layouts, material and finish selections, and documents generated from them (bills of quantities, drawings, renders, proposals).

**Professional (firm) data.** For contractor and design-firm accounts: firm name and branding, rate books, pricing entries, imported quotations, corrections, approvals and markups ("Firm Commercial Data"). We treat Firm Commercial Data as confidential business information — see section 4.

**Usage data.** Actions taken on the Platform (e.g. document generation, exports, reviews), timing metrics, and technical logs (IP address, browser type, session identifiers) used for security, support and product improvement.

**What we do not ask for.** We do not collect government ID numbers, payment-card details (payments, when introduced, will be processed by a licensed payment provider that handles card data directly), or any data of children — the Platform is for users 18 and over.

## 2. Why we process it (legal bases)

We process data (a) to **perform our contract** with you — providing the Platform's core functions; (b) with your **consent**, where required by the PDPL; (c) for our **legitimate operational needs** consistent with the PDPL — securing the Platform, preventing abuse, improving the product using aggregated information; and (d) to **comply with law**.

## 3. AI processing disclosure

The Platform uses third-party AI services to process content you provide: floor-plan images and drawings are parsed by machine-vision models, and photographs may be processed to produce design visualisations. This processing is performed to deliver the service you requested. We contractually restrict our AI providers from using your content to train their models where such controls are offered **[confirm current provider terms: Anthropic, Replicate]**. Generated outputs (BoQs, drawings, renders) are estimates and visualisations — see the Terms of Service for the limits of their intended use.

## 4. Firm Commercial Data — confidentiality commitments

- A firm's rate books, quotations and pricing are visible only to that firm's authorised members and are used only to provide the service to that firm.
- We never disclose one firm's rates, quotes or margins to another firm or to homeowner users.
- Aggregated or benchmark figures derived from firm data will only ever be published with the contributing firm's express prior consent.
- Reference pricing shown on the Platform is presented without the identity of the businesses whose transactions informed it.

## 5. Who we share data with

We share data only with the service providers that run the Platform, under contracts limiting their use of it: hosting and delivery **(Vercel)**; database and authentication **(Supabase, on AWS infrastructure)**; AI image and model processing **(Replicate; Anthropic)**; graph database hosting **(Neo4j Aura)**; encrypted backup storage **(Backblaze)**. We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with advertisers. We may disclose data where required by UAE law or a competent authority.

## 6. International transfers

Our providers store and process data on infrastructure that may be located outside the UAE **[confirm regions per provider]**. Where personal data is transferred outside the UAE, we do so consistent with the PDPL's cross-border transfer requirements, relying on providers' contractual safeguards.

## 7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit; backups are encrypted at rest; access to production data is restricted and logged; firm data access is enforced by per-firm authorisation checks. No system is perfectly secure; we will notify affected users and the UAE Data Office of personal-data breaches as required by the PDPL.

## 8. Retention and deletion

We keep your data while your account is active and as needed to provide the service. On verified request, or on account closure, we delete personal data and firm data within **[30/60/90 — decide]** days, except records we must keep by law and encrypted backups, which expire on their fixed rotation schedule (currently within 8 weeks).

## 9. Your rights

Under the PDPL you may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data; object to or restrict certain processing; and request your data in a portable format. Write to **[privacy@rennovaite.fit]**; we respond within the timelines the PDPL requires. You may complain to the UAE Data Office if unsatisfied.
